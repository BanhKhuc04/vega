VEGA

A self-hosted personal assistant for one person's home and desk.

VEGA runs on its owner's own computer and Raspberry Pi. It answers questions about the room (temperature, air), switches the owner's own devices, watches the owner's software builds, and, when the owner connects their Google account, reads their calendar and the sender and subject of new emails to answer questions and show a morning briefing.

VEGA is not a hosted service: there are no other users and no accounts to sign up for. The source code is public at github.com/BanhKhuc04/vega.
