VEGA privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

VEGA is a personal assistant that its owner runs on their own computer and Raspberry Pi. It has a single user, its owner. This page explains what VEGA does with Google user data when the owner connects their Google account.

What VEGA reads

  • Google Calendar (read-only): the title, start time and location of events from today until the end of tomorrow.
  • Gmail (read-only): the sender and subject of unread messages in the Primary inbox from the last day. VEGA requests message headers only (format=metadata) and never downloads the body of an email.

VEGA never sends, deletes, labels or marks email as read, and never creates or changes calendar events.

How it is used

The data is used only to answer the owner's questions ("what's on today?", "any new mail?"), to show the next event and a morning briefing on the owner's own screen, and to read those answers aloud.

Where it is kept

Calendar and mail data is held in memory on the owner's device and refreshed every few minutes; it is not written to disk. The Google sign-in token is stored in a file on the owner's device that only the owner can read. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any server operated for VEGA.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone. If the owner configures an AI provider and asks a free-form question, the titles and subjects needed to answer it may be sent to that provider, which the owner chooses and configures themselves. Answers to the built-in questions are produced on the device without any AI provider.

VEGA's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke VEGA's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the token file from their device.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/BanhKhuc04/vega/issues.
